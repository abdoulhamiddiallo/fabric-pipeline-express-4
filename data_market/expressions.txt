=== PL_Orchestrator ===

@activity('Check batch').output.exists

[Check batch] et [Read batch] Nom de fichier
batch_@{pipeline().parameters.batch_id}.json

[Route by mode] Expression
@or(equals(activity('Read batch').output.firstRow.mode, 'standard'), equals(activity('Read batch').output.firstRow.mode, 'correction'))

[Load batch] batch_id
@activity('Read batch').output.firstRow.batch_id

[Load batch] mode
@activity('Read batch').output.firstRow.mode

[Load batch] file
@activity('Read batch').output.firstRow.file

[Load batch] run_date
@activity('Read batch').output.firstRow.run_date

=== WH_Market : contrôle rapide ===

UNION ALL SELECT 'fact_sales', COUNT(*) FROM dbo.fact_sales
UNION ALL SELECT 'audit_restatements', COUNT(*) FROM dbo.audit_restatements;
